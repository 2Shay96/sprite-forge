Import rules & limits

No media imported.

1080 × 1080 recommended · 1–8192 px per side · 2,000 frames · 512 MB total. Frames sort by the number in each filename. Opaque images are welcome. Animated GIF/APNG/WebP extraction needs browser support; source delays are recorded, but playback uses Animation FPS. The numbered test (Test kits) has four poses labelled 0–3, left/right colour markers and a feet cross. Filenames 1, 2, 3 and 10 test numeric ordering; its gap warning is intentional. Generator settings
Generated views squash, mirror and darken the front artwork. They don't reconstruct hidden sides, and mirroring also flips text and asymmetry.
Orbit camera & notes Same image shows the front art at every angle. Upload real directions when you have matching artwork.
